# Community guidelines

Lithium welcomes constructive participation from users, developers, and
integrators.

- Be respectful and address ideas on their merits.
- Keep discussions relevant and provide evidence for technical claims.
- Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, or expose private information.
- Do not post scams, referral spam, credential requests, or unsolicited wallet
  support messages.
- Never ask another participant for a private key or seed phrase.
- Report security vulnerabilities privately through the affected repository's
  Security tab.

Organization maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation
when these guidelines are violated.
